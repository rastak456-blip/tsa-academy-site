ESL Course

Power Speaking

A Speaking Course That Maximizes Output and Expression

High speaking volume in 1:1 classes, together with Functional English and Presentation Training, concentrates on the ability to think, explain, and deliver in English.

  • 8-Hour Program
  • 6-Hour Program

Recommended For

This Course Suits You If

  • You want to spend as many hours as possible speaking English
  • You want high-intensity speaking training in a short period
  • You want to focus on practical, everyday English expressions
  • You need presentation and delivery skills for study or work

What You Will Learn

Main Learning Content

Speaking output, Functional English, and Presentation are the core of this course.

Speaking & Vocabulary

Expand speaking output and range of expression across many situations and topics.

Listening & Response

Catch the speaker's intent and respond quickly and naturally.

Reading & Discussion

Structure your opinion and supporting reasons from what you read.

Grammar & Writing Skills

Strengthen accurate English sentences and logical expression.

Functional Expressions

Learn high-frequency expressions for real-life situations.

Presentation Skills & Practice

Train step by step from structuring a talk to delivering it.

LEVEL 5+ DEBATE SKILLS

Presentation extends into Debate

  1. Level 1–4Presentation Skills & PracticeStructure a talk and deliver it with confidence.
  2. Level 5+Debate Skills & PracticeBuild an argument logically and persuade others with evidence.
