Level-Up Learning

Communication Skills That Expand With Every Level

At beginner levels, students focus on everyday questions and answers and basic English expressions. From intermediate levels, Discussion, Argumentation, Debate, and Presentation activities strengthen the ability to explain opinions with reasons and deliver them logically.

  1. Level 1–2BeginnerEveryday questions, answers, and basic expressions
  2. Level 3–4Low-IntermediateReal-life expressions and personal experience
  3. Level 5–6IntermediateDiscussion supported by clear reasons
  4. Level 7–8High-IntermediateArgumentation and structured Presentation
  5. Level 9–10AdvancedPersuasive Debate and advanced Presentation
