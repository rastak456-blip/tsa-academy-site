ESL Courses

A course that completes real-life English step by step

TSA ESL courses build Speaking, Listening, Reading, and Grammar & Writing systematically, so students can use what they learn in real communication.

Depending on your learning goal and preferred class format, you can choose Regular ESL, Regular+ ESL, Intensive Speaking, or Power Speaking.

Level-Up Learning

Communication Skills That Expand With Every Level

Beginner levels focus on everyday questions and basic English expressions. From intermediate, Discussion, Argumentation, Debate, and Presentation build the ability to explain opinions with reasons.

  1. BeginnerBasic CommunicationEveryday questions, answers, and basic expressions
  2. Low-IntermediateOpinion SharingSaying what you think about familiar topics
  3. IntermediateDiscussionExplaining an opinion with clear reasons
  4. High-IntermediateArgumentationBuilding a case and answering counterpoints
  5. AdvancedDebate & PresentationPersuading an audience and presenting with structure
