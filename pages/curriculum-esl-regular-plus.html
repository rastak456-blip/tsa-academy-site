Regular+ ESL

An Extended Course Built on Interaction and Group Communication

Students keep building the core areas of English in balance while a larger share of Small Group classes strengthens the ability to exchange opinions, collaborate, and solve problems together.

  • 8-Hour Program

Recommended For

This Course Suits You If

You want to communicate actively with other people in English

You want more collaboration and Discussion alongside general English

You need practice explaining your opinion and responding to others

You prefer classes centred on practical communication

What You Will Learn

Main Learning Content

Conversation, Collaboration, Decision-Making, and Group Communication shape the character of this course.

Core Language Skills

Speaking, Listening, Reading, and Grammar & Writing are strengthened in balance.

Conversation & Interaction

Ask questions and respond naturally across a wide range of topics.

Collaborative Speaking

Share information and work with other students to complete a task.

Decision-Making Task

Compare options and negotiate opinions to reach a group conclusion.

Group Communication

Role-play, information-gap activities, and opinion exchange build genuine communication.

LEVEL 5+ ARGUMENTATION & DEBATE

Added at intermediate level and above

From intermediate levels, Argumentation and Debate activities extend the ability to deliver opinions with evidence and respond logically to other points of view.
