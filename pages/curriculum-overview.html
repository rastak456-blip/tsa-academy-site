Class Types

Building English Through Different Class Formats

From personalized 1:1 lessons to group activities built on real communication, each course offers the class formats that fit its goal.

1:1 Class

Personalized lessons built around your level

Class pace, learning content, and correction style are adjusted to each student's level and weak areas.

Small Group

Question and respond with students at a similar level

Conversation, Collaboration, and Decision-Making activities with a small number of classmates.

Big Group

Share ideas with a wider group of learners

Topic Communication, Discussion, Debate, and Presentation activities.

Elective

Extra study time to review and reinforce

Review class content and work on Vocabulary, Writing, assignments, and individual weak areas.

Required Study Session

Compulsory study sessions in IELTS Guarantee

Vocabulary review, lesson consolidation, assignments, and weak-area support run as required sessions.

REQUIRED, NOT OPTIONAL

Required Study Sessions are not optional Elective classes. They are compulsory study periods in the IELTS Guarantee course.

Level System

Step-by-Step Learning From Your Current Level

A level test on admission diagnoses your current English level, and classes are assigned across Level 1 to Level 10 in line with the CEFR framework. As levels rise, learning expands from basic English expressions to real-life communication, Discussion, Debate, and Presentation.

  1. Level 1–2BeginnerBasic vocabulary and simple sentences
  2. Level 3–4Low-IntermediateReal-life expressions and personal experience
  3. Level 5–6IntermediateOpinions supported by reasons through Discussion
  4. Level 7–8High-IntermediateStructured Discussion and Presentation
  5. Level 9–10AdvancedFluent, persuasive Debate and Presentation
