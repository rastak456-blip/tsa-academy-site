ESL Course

Intensive Speaking

A 1:1 Centred Course That Concentrates on Speaking

A higher share of 1:1 classes expands the time students spend using English themselves, strengthening Listening, Reading, Grammar, and Writing around a speaking core.

  • 8-Hour Program
  • 6-Hour Program

Recommended For

This Course Suits You If

  • English does not come out quickly in real conversation
  • You want speaking confidence and fluency in a short time
  • You prefer focused 1:1 learning
  • You want to improve accuracy along with speaking

What You Will Learn

Main Learning Content

1:1 Speaking Practice, fluency, and accuracy are the core of this course.

Speaking & Vocabulary

Individual speaking practice widens your range of expression and how much you say.

Listening & Response

Strengthen your ability to understand others and respond immediately.

Reading & Discussion

Understand what you read and express your own opinion in English.

Grammar

Build the grammatical accuracy that speaking and writing require.

Writing Skills

Express your ideas logically in sentences and paragraphs.

Conversation & Collaborative Speaking

Apply the English learned in 1:1 classes to real interaction.

Real-Life Interaction

Practice using English more naturally across a range of everyday situations.

LEVEL 5+ DISCUSSION & DEBATE

Added at intermediate level and above

From intermediate levels, Discussion and Debate activities on more complex topics strengthen the ability to explain opinions in detail and respond logically to other perspectives.
