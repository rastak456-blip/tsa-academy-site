POWER SPEAKING

Speak all day · Output before theory

Best for students who

  • Want to speak English, not only study it
  • Are preparing for interviews, overseas work, or English-based tasks
  • Prefer speaking throughout class instead of group-based study
  • Want noticeable speaking improvement in a short period

What you will learn

  • Extended speakingSpeaking for several minutes without translating mentally
  • Fluency trainingShadowing, repetition, and timed responses
  • Role-play & debateInterviews, meetings, negotiations, and disagreements
  • Pronunciation & intonationCorrection of stress, rhythm, and connected speech
  • Immediate correctionCorrecting errors as soon as they occur
