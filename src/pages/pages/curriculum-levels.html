Level 1–10 · CEFR A1–C1

Level System

A ten-step system based on the CEFR

A level test on admission diagnoses your current English level, and classes are assigned across the detailed Level 1 to Level 10 system aligned with the CEFR.

Students start at their current level and expand their English ability according to the learning goals of each step.

Level Placement

Personalized Learning From Your Current Level

  1. Step 1Level AssessmentA level test on admission assesses your current ability in Speaking, Listening, Reading, and Grammar & Writing.
  2. Step 2Level PlacementYour course and class level are assigned from the test result together with your learning goal.
  3. Step 3Level-Matched Learning1:1 and group classes run with materials and content matched to your current level.
  4. Step 4Progress ReviewLearning progress and attainment are reviewed continuously, with guidance on the next stage of study.

Level-Up Learning Path

Ten Levels, Five Learning Stages

Each stage groups two levels, with the CEFR band and the learning activities that open up as you move through it.

  1. Beginner Level 1–2 · CEFR A1

    Basic Expressions · Questions & Answers

    1. Level 1Learn basic vocabulary and simple sentences and use simple expressions.
    2. Level 2Build everyday conversation skills through routine questions and answers.
  2. Low-Intermediate Level 3–4 · CEFR A2

    Real-Life Communication · Experience Sharing

    1. Level 3Expand real-life expressions and exchange basic opinions.
    2. Level 4Describe your thoughts and experiences in more detail across different situations.
  3. Intermediate Level 5–6 · CEFR B1

    Opinion Sharing · Discussion · Writing

    1. Level 5Extend your opinions and begin explaining them with reasons through Discussion.
    2. Level 6Express ideas more logically and accurately and strengthen writing ability.
  4. High-Intermediate Level 7–8 · CEFR B2

    Advanced Discussion · Presentation

    1. Level 7Build structured communication through advanced Discussion and Presentation.
    2. Level 8Develop opinions on complex topics and communicate from different perspectives.
  5. Advanced Level 9–10 · CEFR C1

    Debate · Persuasive Communication · Presentation

    1. Level 9Deliver opinions persuasively on advanced topics with greater accuracy of expression.
    2. Level 10Explain, debate, and present ideas fluently and precisely in a wide range of situations.

Final Class Placement

Class Assignment Based on Your Level Test Result

The level test result determines your starting level and class placement.

  • Even in the same course, the materials, class content, and correction style can differ according to each student's current level and weak areas.
  • The final course and class level are assigned from the level test result together with your learning goal.
