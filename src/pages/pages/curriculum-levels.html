Level 1–10 · CEFR A1–C1

A Ten-Step Level System Based on the CEFR

A level test on admission diagnoses your current English level, and classes are assigned across the detailed Level 1 to Level 10 system aligned with the CEFR.

Students start at their current level and expand their English ability according to the learning goals of each step.

Level Placement

Personalized Learning From Your Current Level

  1. STEP 1. Level Assessment

    Diagnose your current level

    A level test on admission assesses your current ability in Speaking, Listening, Reading, and Grammar & Writing.

  2. STEP 2. Level Placement

    Assign the right course and level

    Your course and class level are assigned from the test result together with your learning goal.

  3. STEP 3. Level-Matched Learning

    Study at your own level

    1:1 and group classes run with materials and content matched to your current level.

  4. STEP 4. Progress Review

    Review progress and next steps

    Learning progress and attainment are reviewed continuously, with guidance on the next stage of study.

Level-Up Learning Path

English Ability That Expands With Every Level

  1. Level 1–2 · A1BeginnerLearn basic vocabulary and simple sentences and build everyday question-and-answer skills.
  2. Level 3–4 · A2Low-IntermediateExpand real-life expressions and describe your thoughts and experiences in more detail.
  3. Level 5–6 · B1IntermediateExtend your opinions through Discussion and explain them more logically and accurately.
  4. Level 7–8 · B2High-IntermediateStructure your thinking through advanced Discussion and Presentation on complex topics.
  5. Level 9–10 · C1AdvancedDeliver opinions fluently and persuasively on advanced topics through debate and presentation.
