A Core Course That Completes Every English Skill in Balance

Speaking, Listening, Reading, and Grammar & Writing are studied in balance, and Small Group and Big Group classes turn what you learn into real communication.

Recommended For

This Course Suits You If

You want to improve every area of English in balance

You want to move from the basics to practical English step by step

What You Will Learn

Main Learning Content

Speaking & Vocabulary

Learn the vocabulary and expressions needed across a range of topics and express your own ideas in English.

Listening & Response

Strengthen your ability to understand what the other person says and respond naturally to the situation.

Reading & Discussion

Identify the key points of different texts and share your own opinion based on what you read.

Grammar & Writing

Apply grammar to real sentences and writing to build accurate English.

Group Communication

Communicate actively with other students through Conversation, Collaborative Speaking, Topic Communication, and Real-Life Interaction.

LEVEL 5+ DISCUSSION & DEBATE

Added at intermediate level and above

From intermediate levels, Discussion and Debate activities strengthen the ability to explain opinions with reasons and express them more logically.
