Pre-IELTS

A Foundation Course for Students Starting IELTS

Learn the basic structure and question types of the IELTS 4 Skills while building both the core English ability IELTS study requires and general communication skills.

  • 1:1 × 5
  • Small Group × 2
  • Big Group × 1

Recommended For

This Course Suits You If

You are preparing for IELTS for the first time

The exam format and question types are still unfamiliar

You need stronger English fundamentals before full exam preparation

You want to improve IELTS and general English together

What You Will Learn

Main Learning Content

This course strengthens exam preparation and general English communication at the same time.

IELTS 4 Skills

Learn the basic question types and approaches for Speaking, Listening, Reading, and Writing.

IELTS Foundation

Study the basic English and core exam concepts that IELTS preparation requires.

ESL Communication

Small Group and Big Group activities build the ability to share opinions, collaborate, and talk about a range of topics in English.
