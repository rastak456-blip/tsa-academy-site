TSA DAILY ROUTINE

A Day Designed for Focus, Practice, and Recovery

TSA combines personalized 1:1 lessons, communication-focused group classes, guided study, meals, and rest into a steady weekday rhythm.

  1. 07:00

    Breakfast & Preparation

    Students begin the day with breakfast and time to organize materials and learning goals.

  2. 08:00

    Morning 1:1 Lessons

    Personalized lessons focus on the skills and corrections each student needs most.

  3. 10:00

    Small-Group Communication

    Students practice listening and speaking with classmates at a similar level.

  4. 12:00

    Lunch & Reset

    A proper break helps students recharge before the afternoon program.

  5. 13:00

    Afternoon 1:1 / Group Lessons

    The afternoon continues with the lesson mix assigned to the student’s course.

  6. 16:00

    Elective Class or Guided Study

    Depending on the program, students join an elective, receive coaching, or complete assigned study.

  7. 18:00

    Dinner

    Students finish formal classes and take time to eat and unwind.

  8. 19:00

    Review, Self-Study & Rest

    The evening can be used for review, vocabulary, assignments, consultation, or personal rest.

A DAY AT TSA

Focused Lessons, Guided Review, and Time to Connect

The weekday rhythm moves between personal instruction, interactive practice, independent review, and restorative breaks.

HOW IT WORKS

Your Timetable Is Built Around Your Course

Lesson types and daily study hours are arranged according to the selected course, level, and learning goals.

Course-Based Assignment

The number of 1:1, group, elective, and study blocks follows the structure of the enrolled course.

Balanced Learning Blocks

Intensive practice is placed alongside meals and breaks so students can sustain concentration throughout the day.

Manager Support

Students can consult their manager about class concerns, daily-life needs, or schedule-related requests.

Room to Recover

Evening review is encouraged, while sufficient personal time helps students maintain a healthy study rhythm.
