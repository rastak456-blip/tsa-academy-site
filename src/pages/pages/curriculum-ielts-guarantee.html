Required Study Sessions

Compulsory Study, Not Optional Electives

The additional study sessions in the Guarantee course are compulsory study periods, not optional Elective classes.

IELTS Vocabulary Review

Repeat and consolidate the IELTS vocabulary covered in core classes.

Core Review

Review the day's classes, complete assignments, and revise writing.

Independent Study

Focused study centred on individual weak areas.

REQUIRED, NOT OPTIONAL

Required Study Sessions are separate from the Elective classes in other courses, and attendance is compulsory.
